# Hi there, I'm Pablo de Ferra

I'm an **M.Sc. graduate in Astrophysics** from the Universidad de La Laguna, working on **CMB polarization and Galactic foregrounds** with the QUIJOTE CMB group at the **Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC)**. I model polarized synchrotron and thermal dust emission with QUIJOTE, WMAP and Planck data, and I study how synchrotron gradients trace the Galactic magnetic field.

---

## Current Research

- **Spatial variations of polarized foregrounds.** Joint fits of the synchrotron and dust SEDs to the auto- and cross-power spectra from 11 to 353 GHz, in four sky regions and three Galactic cuts. On simulations of a known sky, the Wishart likelihood recovers the parameters while a Gaussian likelihood biases them. First-author paper to be submitted to A&A, presented at *Galactic Science and CMB Foregrounds 2026*.
  - [Code of the paper](https://github.com/pablodeferra/Polarized-foregrounds-QUIJOTE-regions)
  - [Extension with free dust temperature and SED moments](https://github.com/pablodeferra/Polarized-foregrounds-QUIJOTE-regions-Td-moments)
- **Synchrotron gradients and the Galactic magnetic field.** Full-sky pipeline for synchrotron intensity and polarization gradients with CHIPASS, S-PASS, QUIJOTE-MFI and BeyondPlanck, developed during a CASSUM internship with Prof. G. V. Panopoulou at Chalmers University of Technology. Manuscript in preparation.
